The Challenge

Sound familiar?

Many churches struggle with disconnected processes that make it hard to truly know and serve their congregation.

Scattered Member Data

Notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups — member information is everywhere except where you need it.

Untracked Attendance

Multiple services, mid-week fellowships, and special events — but no clear picture of who's showing up.

Opaque Giving Records

Tithes and offerings collected manually with limited transparency, making accountability difficult.

Disconnected Follow-Up

New members, absentees, and those in need slip through the cracks without a system to flag and track pastoral care.

Core Modules

Everything your church needs

Six powerful modules working together to help your church run smoothly and care for every member.

Member Management

Complete member profiles with family links, contact info, ministry involvement, and a self-service profile completion flow for new members.

Attendance Tracking

Record attendance for Sunday services (1st, 2nd, 3rd), mid-week meetings, and special events. View trends per service and per member.

Tithes & Offerings

Log every contribution by category — tithes, offerings, pledges, special seeds. Members can view their own giving history.

Communication

Bulk SMS, targeted messages by group or department, birthday greetings, and push notifications — reach your congregation wherever they are.

Pastoral Care

Flag members for follow-up, track visitations, log counselling notes, and ensure no one falls through the cracks.

Reporting & Accountability

Generate attendance, financial, and growth reports. Audit trails keep everything transparent and trustworthy.

Built for Everyone

Who is ChurchOS for?

Five distinct roles, each with their own tailored dashboard and access level.

Church Administrator

Full system control — manage users, configure settings, and oversee all church operations.

Senior Pastor

Bird's-eye view of church health — attendance trends, giving patterns, and flagged members.

Leader / Dept Head

Manage your department's roster, record attendance, and track follow-ups for your group.

Finance Team

Record and categorise giving, generate financial reports, and maintain accountability.

Member

View your own profile, track attendance, see giving history, and stay updated with announcements.
